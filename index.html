Craft & Method

From First Consultation to Final Walkthrough

A documented, predictable milestone process that treats your home with the care of an art museum.

  1. 01

    On-Site Palette & Substrate Review

    We physically test lighting exposure, evaluate stucco or wood integrity, and recommend precise resin chemistry suited to your surfaces.

  2. 02

    Transparent, Itemized Pre-Costing

    No generic per-square-foot guesswork. You receive a firm line-by-line contract detailing materials, primer count, and scheduled start date.

  3. 03

    Clinical Prep & Pure Application

    80% of fine painting is surgical prep. Flooring is hermetically sealed, fixtures encased, and joints caulked before the first stroke begins.

  4. 04

    White-Glove Architectural Walkthrough

    Every perimeter line is inspected under directional raking light. We do not finalize project sign-off until you are thoroughly delighted.
